Shipping Policy

Where We Ship

We currently ship to all 50 US states. We do not ship internationally at this time.

Processing Time

Orders are packed and shipped within 1-2 business days (Monday-Friday, excluding holidays).

Shipping Rates & Delivery

  • Free standard shipping on all orders over $50.
  • Flat rate $5.95 for orders under $50.
  • Standard delivery: 3-7 business days after shipment, depending on your state.

Tracking

A tracking number is sent by email as soon as your order ships, so you can follow your package every step of the way.

Delays & Issues

If your order is delayed or lost in transit, email hello@just4me.store and we will make it right.

Return & Refund Policy

30-Day Happiness Guarantee

If you are not completely satisfied with your purchase, you may return it within 30 days of delivery for a full refund of the purchase price.

How to Start a Return

  1. Email hello@just4me.store with your order number and the item(s) you wish to return.
  2. We will reply within 1 business day with a prepaid return label (for defective or incorrect items) or return instructions.
  3. Ship the item back; once received, your refund is processed within 5-7 business days to your original payment method.

Conditions

  • Items should be unused or gently used. We understand you need to try a product - reasonable use is fine.
  • For hygiene, we cannot accept returns of heavily used makeup products unless they arrived defective.
  • Defective, damaged, or incorrect items are always refunded in full, including return shipping.

Refund Timing

Approved refunds are issued to your original payment method within 5-7 business days after we receive your return.
